🔎 HOW TO PLAY

  1. Teacher presses RANDOM GROUP. Every group and pet mission appears only once.
  2. Stage 1: 3–4 students wear colored badges. Move left or right until the question chunks are in the correct order. Hold for 2 seconds.
  3. The Question Captain checks the grammar and meaning.
  4. Stage 2: Discuss, then show at least 3 open palms under the same answer. Keep Team Power synchronized for 5 seconds.
  5. A wrong answer becomes a silly monster. Try again—there is no penalty.

Camera tip: Place the laptop 2–3 metres away in landscape view. Use bright colored badge cards near each player’s chest.
