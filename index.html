DETECTIVE FIELD GUIDE

Two challenges. One team.

  1. Wear your player colors. Red, blue, green and yellow badges go on your chest. Stand apart, with your upper bodies and badges in view.
  2. Build the question. Each player carries one word card. Move left and right until the sentence is correct. Hold for 2 seconds!
  3. Power up your answer. Discuss, then show open palms to the camera. Move palms underneath your answer. At least 3 palms must agree for 5 continuous seconds.

Extra teammates are Question Captains. The color mission needs only 3 word players. Wrong answers get a silly surprise—then you try again!

Teacher setup: landscape screen, bright even light, clear floor space, solid colored chest badges. Keep captains outside the camera during calibration.
